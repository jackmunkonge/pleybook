MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made between Tidewater Development Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (the Supplier), and Alder Health Trust, a body registered in England and Wales (the Customer).

1. Definitions and Services
1.1 In this Agreement "Services" means the services described in the relevant Order, and "Fees" means the amounts payable for them.
1.2 "Deliverables" means all materials, reports and software produced by the Supplier for the Customer, and "Customer Data" means data supplied by or on behalf of the Customer.
1.3 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified personnel, and in accordance with the relevant Order.

2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The Customer shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days. Overdue amounts, including amounts disputed in good faith, accrue interest at three per cent (3%) per month.

3. Data
3.1 The Supplier may appoint sub-processors of its choice without notice to the Customer and is not required to hold any security certification when processing the Customer's personal data.

4. Intellectual Property
4.1 The Customer grants the Supplier a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide licence to use the Customer Data for any purpose, including the development of the Supplier's other products.

5. Liability and Indemnity
5.1 The Supplier's liability under this Agreement is capped at the Fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months. The Customer's liability to the Supplier under this Agreement is unlimited.
5.2 Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its breach of applicable law or its negligence, provided that the indemnified party gives prompt notice, allows the indemnifying party to control the defence, and the claim is not settled without the indemnified party's consent.

6. Term and Confidentiality
6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice. On termination the Customer shall pay only for Services rendered up to the termination date.
6.2 Each party shall use the other's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement and shall keep it confidential for five (5) years after termination (and trade secrets for as long as they remain secret). This does not apply to information that is public, already known, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.

7. General
7.1 Neither party is in breach of this Agreement for any delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other party promptly and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.
7.2 The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, agency or employment relationship between them.
7.3 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes all earlier discussions and understandings.
7.4 If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.
7.5 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
