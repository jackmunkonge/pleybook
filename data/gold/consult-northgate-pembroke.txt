CONSULTANCY AGREEMENT

This Consultancy Agreement is made between Northgate Advisory Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (the Supplier), and Pembroke Estates plc, a company registered in England and Wales (the Customer).

1. Services
1.1 In this Agreement "Services" means the services described in the relevant Order, and "Fees" means the amounts payable for them.
1.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified personnel, and in accordance with the relevant Order.
1.3 The Customer shall give the Supplier the information and access reasonably required to perform the Services.

2. Fees
2.1 The Customer shall pay all invoices within ten (10) days of receipt. Interest of two per cent (2%) per week accrues on all overdue amounts, and the Customer may not withhold any disputed amount.

3. Intellectual Property
3.1 The Customer owns all Deliverables and all Customer Data. The Supplier retains its pre-existing materials and grants the Customer a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use any of them that are embedded in the Deliverables.

4. Confidentiality
4.1 The Supplier may use the Customer's Confidential Information for the benefit of its other clients and in its marketing materials, and its confidentiality obligations end on termination.

5. Liability and Indemnity
5.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other for any indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profits, loss of revenue or loss of data, however arising.
5.2 The Customer shall indemnify the Supplier and its officers against all claims and losses arising from the Services, however caused, including the Supplier's own negligence.

6. General
6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ontario, Canada, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Toronto.
6.2 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand or by recorded post to the address of the receiving party stated in this Agreement.
6.3 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes all earlier discussions and understandings.
6.4 The Supplier shall maintain professional indemnity and public liability insurance with a reputable insurer throughout the term of this Agreement.
6.5 If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.
