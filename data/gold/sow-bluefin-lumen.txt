STATEMENT OF WORK AND SERVICES TERMS

This Statement of Work is made between Bluefin Digital Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (the Supplier), and Lumen Energy plc, a company registered in England and Wales (the Customer).

1. Services
1.1 In this Agreement "Services" means the services described in the relevant Order, and "Fees" means the amounts payable for them.
1.2 "Deliverables" means all materials, reports and software produced by the Supplier for the Customer, and "Customer Data" means data supplied by or on behalf of the Customer.
1.3 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified personnel, and in accordance with the relevant Order.
1.4 The Customer shall give the Supplier the information and access reasonably required to perform the Services.

2. Fees
2.1 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt. The Customer may withhold any amount disputed in good faith, and no late fee or interest shall accrue on amounts so disputed.

3. Intellectual Property
3.1 The Supplier owns all Deliverables created under this Statement of Work. The Customer receives a non-exclusive licence to use them for the duration of the Agreement only.

4. Confidentiality
4.1 The Customer shall keep the Supplier's Confidential Information confidential indefinitely, but the Supplier may disclose the Customer's Confidential Information to any third party it chooses.

5. Termination
5.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice. On termination the Customer shall pay only for Services rendered up to the termination date.
5.2 The Supplier may suspend the Services and terminate this Agreement immediately on any late payment, and no prepaid Fees are refundable. The Customer has no corresponding right to terminate for breach.

6. Liability
6.1 The maximum aggregate liability of either party to the other for all claims under this Agreement is limited to an amount equal to the charges paid or payable during the previous twelve (12) months.

7. General
7.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand or by recorded post to the address of the receiving party stated in this Agreement.
7.2 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes all earlier discussions and understandings.
7.3 If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.
7.4 A person who is not a party to this Agreement has no right to enforce any of its terms.
