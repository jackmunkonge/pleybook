SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Software-as-a-Service Agreement is made between Pinecrest Software Inc. (the Supplier) and Harbour Foods Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (the Customer).

1. Subscription
1.1 In this Agreement "Services" means the services described in the relevant Order, and "Fees" means the amounts payable for them.
1.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified personnel, and in accordance with the relevant Order.

2. Availability
2.1 The Supplier targets 95% availability of the Service in each month. Failure to meet this target does not entitle the Customer to any service credit, refund or right to terminate.

3. Fees
3.1 The subscription Fees increase automatically by twelve per cent (12%) on each anniversary of the Effective Date without notice.
3.2 The Customer shall pay each invoice in full within seven (7) days of the invoice date. The Supplier may suspend the Service immediately if any payment is late.

4. Data
4.1 The Supplier shall process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions under the Customer's data processing agreement, shall maintain security certified to SOC 2 or ISO 27001, shall not appoint any sub-processor without the Customer's prior approval, and shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of it.

5. Term
5.1 Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience. If the Customer stops using the Service before the end of the three-year term, it must still pay all Fees for the remaining term.

6. Liability
6.1 Each party's total aggregate liability arising under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

7. General
7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
7.2 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand or by recorded post to the address of the receiving party stated in this Agreement.
7.3 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes all earlier discussions and understandings.
7.4 Neither party shall issue any press release about this Agreement without the prior written approval of the other party.
